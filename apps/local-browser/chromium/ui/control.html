Welcome

Paperclip agents can work in this browser on your behalf. Two steps get them going:

  1. Allow access. Grant the browser permissions agents need. You can revoke any of them later.
  2. Pair with Paperclip. Open the Local Browser page in Paperclip and paste its pairing link on the Overview.
